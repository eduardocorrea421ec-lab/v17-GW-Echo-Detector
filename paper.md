# Summary

The v17 GW Echo Detector is an open-source Python pipeline designed to search for gravitational wave echoes following binary black hole mergers.

# Statement of Need

The detection of gravitational waves by LIGO and Virgo has opened a new observational window into strong gravity. While standard General Relativity predicts that black holes have absorbing horizons and produce no echoes, several beyond-GR theories predict echoes appearing 0.1 to 1 second after the main merger.

# State of the field

Previous work on echo detection includes the seminal work of Abedi et al. [@Abedi2017] and Conklin et al. [@Conklin2018]. Westerweck et al. [@Westerweck2018] provided a more conservative analysis.
